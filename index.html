In use at a festival

CompSuite

Dance festivals and competitions

A complete entry system for dance festivals, built for the North Cornwall Dance Festival and now being rolled out across the wider festival and competition network.

  • Replaces Word documents, Google Forms and Jotform
  • Keeps entries organised in one place
  • Competition management made simple
Visit compsuite.uk

Classready

Dance and theatre schools

The admin home for dance and theatre schools. Timetables, registers, forms and notices live in one place instead of across different systems.

  • Classes, schedules and registers
  • Forms, notices and contact with students
  • Its own branded landing page for each school
  • Free to run, with no monthly or setup fees
Visit classready.live

Showready

Dance schools, theatre groups and drama societies

From first rehearsal to final bow. Cast details, emergency contacts, rehearsal dates, costumes, safeguarding policies, tickets and parent communications, all in one place.

  • A public show page for each production
  • Guardian and performer details, cast lists, safeguarding
  • Ticket sales
  • Student details flow in from ClassReady, with no retyping
Start a free trial

Marginalia

Authors and editors

Proofreading software for authors and editors. Upload a Word document or PDF, choose UK Oxford or US English, and get a clear report with every note tied to an exact quotation from your manuscript.

  • Your work stays private and the copyright stays yours
  • Your manuscript is never used to train AI
  • The first chapter of every manuscript is proofread free
Visit marginaliaproof.com

Flash Quiz

GCSE students

Born from finding a better way to help a 15-year-old with GCSE revision. Personalised flashcards, intelligent quizzes and AI-powered feedback make revision feel more like a game than a chore.

  • Covers all GCSE subjects, plus custom subjects
  • Points, achievements and progress tracking
  • Feedback on your answers
Visit flash-quiz.com
